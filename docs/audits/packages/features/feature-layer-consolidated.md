## 4. Contradictions and differences

1. **CF-24, collection duplication.**
   - A:FA-24 calls the documents/dashboards read and mapping accidental duplication to share (re-classifying the earlier D15 "intentional").
   - B §C says keep the three `toCollectionItem` mappers separate: they have different routes and details, and sharing saves about 10 lines each.
   - **Code check:** `apps/web/src/features/documents/lib/collection-item/collection-item.ts` and `.../dashboards/lib/collection-item/collection-item.ts` are identical once the resource name is substituted (empty diff). `get-documents.ts` and `get-dashboards.ts` differ only in the fixture constant. The spaces mapper does differ: it uses a contents count, and it lives in features-spaces.
   - **Resolution:** both statements are factually right about different pairs. B is right that spaces differs; A is right that documents and dashboards are identical. Whether to share is a design choice, not a factual dispute: **owner decision (A:K4)**. Documents and Dashboards naming is still "strong working term" in the glossary (B:FA10), which argues for deferring any shared package until naming settles.
2. **CF-03, severity.** A rated it Low ("dead contract"); B rated it Medium (an enabled destructive button after expiry). The card source confirms B's user-visible impact, so the consolidated rating is **Medium**. A's correction ("read it in the card") is compatible with B's more specific recommendation (drop the button and make settings the primary action).
3. **Verification results differ but don't conflict.** A reports `boundaries` and `deps` passing; B reports them failing. A ran on clean `9ee59e3c`; B included the uncommitted server work, which caused its failures. Both are accurate for their trees. At `192579bf` the causes look fixed by source (CF-26), but this hasn't been re-run.
4. **CF-02, remedy shape.**
   - A:K1 recommends a real `./frontend/next` entry with host-supplied navigation via props or slots.
   - B:FA2 recommends one host-bound navigation provider or props, and leaves the capability's home to the frontend agent.
   - These are compatible. The open question is where the navigation capability lives, and that needs **frontend reconciliation** before an owner decision.
5. **Ownership of copy for server outcomes.** B:FA4 argues outcome-to-copy mapping belongs to features. A does not address it, though its ownership map lists the app action files as the hosts. Not a contradiction, but an unresolved rule (CF-11).

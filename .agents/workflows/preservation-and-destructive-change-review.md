# Preservation and destructive-change review

1. Read `docs/engineering/preservation-policy.md`.
2. Before deleting, overwriting, rewriting history, or changing an external setting, state the exact scope and the recovery path, and look at the target first.
3. Prefer archiving to deleting. Rewriting or force-pushing history, changing repository settings, and closing or reopening issues need the human's explicit instruction for that action.
4. After any change to shared or external state, confirm the resulting state and report it.
